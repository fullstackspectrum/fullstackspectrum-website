Sbomly

SBOM diffs

Diff two SBOMs (CycloneDX or SPDX) and flag what deserves review: integrity, copyleft, licence and supplier changes. Terminal, PR comment, JSON and offline HTML output, with a fail-on switch to gate CI.

about

Security tooling,
end to end.

Full-Stack-Spectrum is where I build and publish open-source tools for DevSecOps teams — the stuff I wished existed when staring at a wall of scanner JSON.

The focus is on turning noisy data into decisions: which CVEs actually matter, where the blast radius ends, and which policies are doing the work.

Everything ships the easy way — Homebrew, PyPI or a single Docker container — and the Cloudsmith tools are community projects, not affiliated with Cloudsmith.
